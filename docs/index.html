Overview

Oceanography meets
natural language

FloatChat is a conversational data exploration platform that makes accessing large-scale ARGO oceanographic datasets as easy as typing a question. No scripts. No NetCDF headaches. Just answers.

💬
Natural Language Queries
Ask questions in plain English. FloatChat translates them into structured data operations across ARGO NetCDF datasets.
📊
Interactive Visualizations
Get instant graphs, charts, and plots — powered by Plotly and Matplotlib — directly in the chat interface.
🤖
Dual AI Agents
Switch between Ocean RAG for dataset-grounded answers and MarineAI Bot for open-ended marine life conversations.
⚡
Real-Time Data Feed
Live SST readings and oceanographic feeds surface right in the dashboard — always fresh, always current.
🗺️
Float Path Tracking
Visualize the trajectories of individual ARGO floats across the Indian Ocean over time.
🔬
Analytics Dashboard
Aggregate views, depth profiles, temperature gradients, and salinity trends — all from a single interface.
Dual Agents

Two brains.
One ocean.

The chat widget in FloatChat runs two specialized agents simultaneously — each designed for a different kind of conversation.

Agent 01
Ocean RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation grounded in your pre-fed ARGO datasets. Ask about specific float measurements, depth profiles, temperature anomalies, or salinity trends — and get answers directly derived from your data, not hallucinated.
🌊
Agent 02
MarineAI Bot
A free-form LLM assistant specialized in marine biology and ocean science. Ask about species, ecosystems, climate impact on oceans, or any open-ended scientific question. No dataset needed — just curiosity.
